It started at the family stove.

Tanushree Mhatre learned to cook standing on a stool beside her grandmother, grinding masalas and tasting every pot. Friends kept asking for her dishes at every gathering, so she opened her kitchen to everyone.

Today every order is still cooked in small batches, with spices roasted and ground the same week. The recipes haven't changed. Only the number of tables they reach has.

Tanushree Mhatre, Cook and Founder
